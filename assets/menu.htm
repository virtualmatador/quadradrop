How to Play
  1. Press the "Play" button to open the game board.
  2. The game starts at paused state. Press resume button to play.
  3. Use one of these methods to move and rotate the falling piece and complete horizontal lines.
    • Swipe to move, tap to rotate, long press to drop.
    • Use the on-screen controls.
    • Use left, right, and down arrow keys to move, up arrow key to rotate, and D to drop.
    • Tap Explode, press E, or swipe up to spend one Quadra. The current piece's four cells fill empty spaces from the bottom center outward.
  4. Clearing several lines at once awards more points; clearing four is a Quadra.
  5. Tap the back button at the top-right corner to return to this page.
End of Game
  • The game ends when a new piece cannot enter the board.
  • Advancing levels increases the speed of the falling piece.
  • To play again, press the "Restart" button on the game-over screen.
  • Restarting the game clears your score, line count, and Quadra count.
